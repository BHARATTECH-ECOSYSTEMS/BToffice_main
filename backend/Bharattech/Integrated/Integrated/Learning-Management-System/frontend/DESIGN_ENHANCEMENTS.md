## Design Principles Applied

1. **Modern Aesthetics**: Clean, minimal design with premium touches
2. **Visual Hierarchy**: Clear distinction between elements
3. **Micro-interactions**: Engaging hover and click effects
4. **Color Psychology**: Orange for energy, Purple for premium
5. **Responsive Design**: Works beautifully on all screen sizes
6. **Performance**: Smooth animations that don't impact performance
